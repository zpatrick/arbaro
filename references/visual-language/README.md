# Visual language references

These images are the visual standard for Arbaro. The target is restrained,
professional and dramatic: bold silhouettes, tapering organic lines, controlled
negative space, asymmetry, scrollwork, and detail that remains subordinate to a
clear overall form.

The JBenart beach-art references originate from:

- [JBenart — Beach Art portfolio](https://jbenart.com/en/portfolio-beach-art-en/)

The first five images were supplied separately by the user. The remaining
images were supplied from the JBenart portfolio. They are kept here as design
references; ownership remains with their respective creators.

Do not use the images as shipped game assets. Translate their visual principles
into original procedural artwork.
